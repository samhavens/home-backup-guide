# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Users
Bob and Roberta, older adults reading on phones, and family helping compare home backup options in Calabasas.
## Product Purpose
Turn answers to a one-question-at-a-time quiz into an actionable backup plan for Bob and Roberta. Start with modest backup, explicit cooling needs, retirement affordability, and ease of use. Solar must justify its additional cost.
## Evidence on Hand
The user's supplied guide and verified primary sources for health, rebate, tax, and contact details. Household usage, roof condition, outage history, and eligibility are unknown.
## Accessibility & Inclusion
Large adjustable text, high contrast, generous touch targets, phone layout, keyboard access, ordinary document scrolling, descriptive links, reduced motion, print support.
## Deployment and privacy
Public GitHub Pages under samhavens, as requested. No sign-in. Quiz answers remain in page memory and are cleared on reload. Only the text-size preference is stored in the browser. Users can copy or print their completed plan.
## Decision constraints
Recommend a next action and quote scope rather than asserting equipment sizing or eligibility. Unknown answers and affordability mismatches lead to specific assessment or assistance steps. Keep the full guide as optional background.
