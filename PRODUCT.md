# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Users
Bob and Roberta, older adults reading on phones, and family helping compare home backup options in Calabasas.
## Product Purpose
Explain the supplied backup-power decision guide directly to Bob and Roberta. Start with modest backup, explicit cooling needs, retirement affordability, and ease of use. Solar must justify its additional cost.
## Evidence on Hand
The user's supplied guide and verified primary sources for health, rebate, tax, and contact details. Household usage, roof condition, outage history, and eligibility are unknown.
## Accessibility & Inclusion
Large adjustable text, high contrast, generous touch targets, phone layout, keyboard access, ordinary document scrolling, descriptive links, reduced motion, print support.
## Open decisions
User is being asked about progressive disclosure and sign-in requirements. Static HTML is an implementation assumption because the guide needs no server or accounts.
